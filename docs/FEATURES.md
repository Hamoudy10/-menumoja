# MenuMoja — Everything the System Can Do (in Simple Words)

Think of MenuMoja as a **smart waiter, cashier, cash register, menu, and marketing team — all in one app**.
It runs on a phone, tablet, or computer. No special machines needed.

---

## 1. The Digital Menu (what customers see when they scan)

**Imagine a menu that is a website instead of paper.**

- Every restaurant gets its own menu link, like `menumoja.app/your-restaurant`.
- **QR codes**: small square barcodes. You print one for each table and one for the entrance.
- A customer points their phone camera at the QR code → the menu opens instantly. **No app to download.**
- The menu shows your dishes, photos, prices, and descriptions.
- It looks beautiful and works on every phone.
- **3 languages**: English, Swahili, and Arabic. Customers pick their language.
- Customers can **add items to their cart** and **place the order** from their phone.
- **Takeaway**: customers who are not sitting at a table can type their name and phone number so you know who the order belongs to.
- **Specials & Offers**: you can show "Today's Special", discounts, events, and giveaways on the menu.
- Your **logo, colors, and fonts** appear on the menu — it looks like YOUR restaurant, not a generic app.

## 2. The Chef AI Assistant (a robot that helps customers)

**A friendly robot that answers customers' questions on the menu.**

- Customer asks: "What's popular?" → the AI suggests your best dishes.
- Customer asks: "Do you have vegetarian food?" → the AI lists them.
- Customer asks: "Is this halal?" or "How spicy is this?" → the AI answers from YOUR menu.
- Customer can ask general questions too — jokes, recipes, math.
- The AI can **add dishes straight to the customer's cart**.
- Works in English and Swahili.

## 3. Orders (what happens when a customer orders)

**The moment a customer presses "Place Order", your staff see it.**

- **Kitchen Display**: a big screen in the kitchen shows every order as a ticket — the dishes, quantities, special instructions ("no onions!"), and how long the order has been waiting. If an order takes too long, the ticket turns red.
- Kitchen staff press buttons: **Start Preparing → Mark Ready → Mark Served**.
- **Waiter Dashboard**: waiters see all active orders on their phone, can mark food as served, see which tables are occupied/free on a **floor plan**, and submit customer complaints.
- **Table status**: when an order arrives at a table, the table automatically becomes "Occupied". When the customer pays, it becomes "Free" again. The floor plan shows this live.
- Takeaway orders show the **customer's name and phone number** so you know who to call.

## 4. Payments (getting paid)

**Money made easy — and safe.**

- **Cash**: the cashier enters the amount received and the system shows the change.
- **M-Pesa STK Push**: the customer gets a payment request on their phone, enters their PIN, and you get the money **straight into YOUR till or paybill**. MenuMoja never touches your money.
- **Card**: card payments supported.
- **ETR Receipts**: receipts that satisfy Kenya's tax rules (KRA), with your business PIN and VAT breakdown. Can be printed on a small receipt printer (80mm thermal).
- **Receipt history**: find any old receipt by date, payment method, table, or customer name — and print it again.
- **Shift management**: cashiers open a shift, record how much cash they have, and close the shift with a full count — so you know exactly what should be in the till.
- **Discounts, tips, service charge, split bills, voids**: all handled, with a record of everything.

## 5. The Floor Plan (knowing your tables)

**A drawing of your restaurant on the screen.**

- Every table is a shape on the drawing (round, square, rectangle, booth...).
- You can **drag tables** to their real position, resize them, rotate them.
- **Zones**: color-coded areas like "Inside", "Patio", "Rooftop".
- The plan shows each table's live status: green = free, blue = occupied, orange = preparing food, green pulsing = food ready.
- Tap a table to see its orders.
- Waiter and cashier dashboards share this same live floor plan.

## 6. Menu Management (changing your menu)

**No more printing new menus every time.**

- Add, edit, and remove dishes instantly. Changes appear on customers' phones immediately.
- For each dish you can set: name, price, photo, description, prep time, calories, spice level, ingredients, allergens, and dietary flags (halal, vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free).
- Localized names: the same dish in English, Swahili, and Arabic.
- **AI writes your descriptions** and can even generate dish photos.
- Organize dishes into categories and drag them into order.

## 7. Promotions (bringing customers in)

- Create **Specials** (a dish on offer), **Offers** (discounts), **Events** (live music night), and **Giveaways**.
- Link a promotion to a dish with a special price — customers see the old price crossed out.
- Schedule them with start/end dates, pause them anytime.
- They appear on the customer menu automatically.

## 8. Analytics & Reports (knowing your business)

**See how your restaurant is really doing.**

- Today's revenue and number of orders at a glance.
- Which dishes sell the most.
- How customers pay (cash vs M-Pesa vs card).
- Trends over time (hourly, daily, monthly).
- QR scan counts (how many people scanned your codes).

## 9. Staff Management (your team)

- Add staff with roles: **waiter**, **cashier**, **kitchen**.
- Each staff member logs in with a PIN.
- They see only what their job needs.
- Every action is recorded (who did what).

## 10. Cameras / Surveillance (extra safety, Premium option)

- Connect your CCTV cameras and watch them from the dashboard.
- AI checks food quality on camera and alerts you about problems.

## 11. Settings & Appearance (making it yours)

- Your restaurant's name, phone, address, KRA PIN, business registration numbers.
- **Upload your logo and cover photo** — they appear on your customer menu.
- Choose your brand colors, gradients, and fonts.
- Staff dashboards use your brand colors too — everything looks like one system.
- **M-Pesa setup**: type in your till/paybill number and passkey so payments work with YOUR number.

## How the parts talk to each other

```
Customer's phone  →  Menu website  →  MenuMoja servers  →  Kitchen screen
       ↑                                                 ↓
       └──────────  M-Pesa payment  ──────────  Cashier (POS)
```

- The **menu website** is what customers see.
- The **servers** (cloud) keep everything in sync instantly.
- The **staff screens** (kitchen, waiter, cashier) update live the second an order is placed.

## Is it safe?

- Staff use PINs; owners use passwords.
- Every money action is recorded.
- Payments go directly to the restaurant's own M-Pesa number.
- Your data is stored securely in the cloud (and can be backed up daily).

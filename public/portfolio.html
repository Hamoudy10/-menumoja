01 · About

About Menu Moja

Menu Moja is a restaurant technology platform designed for restaurants in Kenya. It helps you manage daily operations, accept orders and payments, coordinate kitchen activity, understand business performance, and build stronger relationships with your customers.

Menu Moja works in two ways: as a complete restaurant platform — or as a complement to the POS you already use. Start where you have gaps; grow from there.

05 · An important note

We don't require you to replace your existing system

Already using a POS? No problem.

Menu Moja can be introduced alongside your existing systems where appropriate — starting with QR ordering, digital menus, customer engagement, analytics and other areas where your current system may have gaps. You keep what works. You add what's missing.

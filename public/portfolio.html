Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to replace my existing POS?

No. Menu Moja can work alongside your current system — adding QR ordering, digital menus, customer engagement and analytics where they are missing.

Can Menu Moja handle QR ordering?

Yes. Customers scan a QR code at the table and browse the digital menu on their phone, in English, Swahili or Arabic.

Does Menu Moja support M-Pesa?

Yes. M-Pesa payments are integrated into the ordering and payment flow.

Can customers order from their phones?

Yes. Customers can browse the menu, build a cart and place an order from their phone after scanning the QR code.

Does Menu Moja include a kitchen display?

Yes. Orders reach the kitchen automatically through the Kitchen Display System, with preparation tracking.

Can I manage restaurant staff?

Yes. Staff accounts with roles (cashier, waiter, kitchen, manager) and PIN-based access are supported.

Can Menu Moja work with multiple branches?

The platform is designed to support multiple restaurants and branches.

How difficult is setup?

Setup is guided: tell us about your restaurant, configure the menu, set up tables and staff, configure payments and go live.

Do you provide training?

Yes. We help restaurants understand, configure and adopt the system, including training your team.

What happens if the internet goes down?

Offline-first ordering queues are built in so orders and payments can be recorded and synced when the connection returns.

How much does Menu Moja cost?

We offer flexible plans for different restaurants. Contact us for a plan tailored to your restaurant.
